About Us
Location:
Ssaza Road, Katwe-Butego Sub-County, Masaka District (about 87 miles from Kampala).
Background:
Founded by local volunteers and teachers to support vulnerable, orphaned, and disadvantaged children from local communities.
Operations:
Functions as both a day and boarding primary school, serving hundreds of pupils.
Mission:
To deliver a high-quality, inclusive primary education that inspires a lifelong love of learning and critical thinking.
Vision:
To be a leading primary school known for academic excellence, dedicated teachers, and well-rounded students.
